Transfer Clash Support

Transfer Clash is a real-time football trivia game where players compete to identify footballers who have played for both selected clubs.

Common Issues

Connection or matchmaking problems

Transfer Clash uses real-time online connections for matches. If you have trouble joining a match, please check your internet connection, close and reopen the app, and try again.

Guest account issues

You can play as a guest without creating an account. If you later create an account, some guest account features may be upgraded depending on the app version and account status.

Leaderboard and rating

Transfer Clash uses a competitive rating system. Your rating may change after completed matches depending on the result and match performance.

Match history

Match history is linked to your account. If your history does not appear, please make sure you are logged in with the correct account.

Reporting a Bug

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Your username or guest account name, if available
  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, if possible
